# Voice

Layer 3 rule book. Who we write for and how narration sounds.

## Voice

We write for a curious adult with no background in the subject and no patience for being flattered. Second person. Short declaratives. One idea per sentence.

- Say the thing, then say why it matters. Never the reverse.
- A word a reader would have to look up is replaced or defined in the same breath.
- Analogies are allowed once each. Two analogies for one idea means the first one failed.

## Honesty

- A claim with no source line in the brief does not go in the narration. It is cut, not softened into "some people say".
- Uncertainty is stated plainly: what is settled, what is disputed, what nobody knows.
- Never imply a study, a number or an expert that the brief does not name.

## Hard Constraints

- No exclamation marks.
- No em dashes.
- No rhetorical questions in the opening beat.
- No "in this video we will". Start with the thing itself.
- Runtime is a constraint, not a target. Cutting a beat is better than talking faster.

## When a rule is wrong

Write it into `../_log/FORGE-PROPOSALS.md` with the line of narration that broke on it. A human edits this file.
