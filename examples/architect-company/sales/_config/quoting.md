# Quoting

Layer 3 rule book, binding inside sales only. Prices are not here. They are in `../../_config/rate-card.md`, which is the company rule book and the only home for a figure.

## Building a quote

1. Name the item exactly as the rate card names it.
2. Count hire days. Five or more is quoted at the week rate.
3. Add the deposit from the card. Say it is held, not charged.
4. Add delivery if it is inside the town boundary. Outside it, ask the owner.
5. Put the date on it. A written quote stands for 14 days.

## Chasing

One follow up after 3 days. One more after 7. Then stop and leave the quote open. Nobody is chased twice in the same week.

Past 14 days a quote is requoted from the card, never extended. Prices move and a stale quote is a promise we did not mean to make.

## What sales never does

- Never quotes an item that is not on the rate card.
- Never discounts to close. A discount request is a call, and the quoted price holds until the owner answers.
- Never tells a customer a tool is available without the diary open in front of it.

## When a rule is wrong

Log a call line in `../../_log/LOOP-LEDGER.md` with the quote it broke on. A human edits this file.
