# Listings

Layer 3 rule book, binding inside marketing only.

## What every listing must say

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name | the same words the sales rate card uses, exactly |
| Day and week price | copied from the sales rate card on the day of the edit |
| Deposit | stated, and stated as held, never as part of the price |
| Delivery | inside the town boundary only. Outside is "ask us", never a figure |
| Availability | a date, or the words "ask us". Never "available now" written in advance |

## Rules

- Marketing never sets a price. It shows one. A price that looks wrong is a question for sales, and the listing is left alone until sales answers.
- A photo shows the tool that is hired, not a similar model.
- A listing edited without a price check is not edited. Check first, then edit.

## The newsletter

One a month. One offer at most, and only an offer sales has already agreed. No countdowns, no urgency language, no exclamation marks.

Nothing goes out without the owner reading it. Drafted is not sent, and the report says which.

## When a rule is wrong

Log a call line in `../../_log/LOOP-LEDGER.md` naming the listing. A human edits this file.
