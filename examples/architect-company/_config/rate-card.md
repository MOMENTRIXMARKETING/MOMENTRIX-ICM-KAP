# Rate card

Layer 3 rule book. The one home for what a hire costs. Sales quotes from it and marketing shows it. Neither invents a figure and neither keeps a second copy.

## The card

| Item | Day | Week | Deposit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 inch grinder | 24 | 96 | 60 |
| Wet tile saw | 45 | 180 | 150 |
| Plate compactor | 65 | 260 | 200 |
| Scaffold tower, 4m | 55 | 220 | 250 |

Dollars, delivery excluded. Delivery inside the town boundary is 20 each way. Outside it, quote nothing until the owner gives a figure.

## Rules

- A week is 5 hire days. A hire of 5 days or more is quoted at the week rate, never at 5 day rates.
- The deposit is held, not earned. It is money we owe back until the tool is back and checked.
- An item not on this card has no price. That is a question for the owner, never a guess and never a discount to close a quote.
- A figure here is changed by the owner and nobody else. Copying it into a listing or a quote is fine. Editing the copy instead of the card is the mistake this file exists to stop.

## When a rule is wrong

Log a call line in `../_log/LOOP-LEDGER.md` with the quote or listing it broke on. A human edits this file.
