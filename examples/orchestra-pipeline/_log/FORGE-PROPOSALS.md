# FORGE PROPOSALS

What the forge proposes, waiting on a human decision. The forge never edits a rule book and the logger never judges.

## Proposals

One block per proposal, appended below Authority. Quote the ledger evidence and name the smallest test that would prove the change. No proposals are open in this worked example.

## Authority

- **safe fix** - deterministic and reversible, applied by the agent and then reported.
- **mechanical report** - a script found it and must not fix it. A human decides.
- **judgment report** - a model opinion. Always a proposal, never a direct change.

A proposal with no evidence is an opinion, not a proposal. Paste the ledger lines.
