# Request brief

Source: [materialised example request](example-request.md)

## Deliverable

A short evidence-linked orientation report for the fictional neighbourhood workshop.

## Required content

State the purpose, step-free access, arrival and preparation instructions, and unresolved details. Use only the public-safe raw captures and the compiled wiki article.

## Boundaries

Keep every factual claim tied to an evidence row. Do not invent missing details. No invitation, calendar change, booking, message, purchase, or other external action is authorised.
