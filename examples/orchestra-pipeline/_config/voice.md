# Voice

Layer 3 rule book for public prose in this fictional example.

## Voice

Write for a curious adult with no bicycle repair experience. Use short declaratives and plain words. State the useful fact first, then its practical consequence.

## Honesty

Use only claims carried by the evidence brief. Keep the source's surface form for dates, times, numbers, and quotes. If the source leaves a detail open, state that detail as open. Never invent an organiser, venue, price, credential, or capability.

## Hard Constraints

- No em dashes.
- No exclamation marks.
- No company or personal data.
- No urgency, sales pressure, or invented testimonial.
- No message, invitation, booking, or purchase without separate human approval.

## When a rule is wrong

Append the conflicting passage and the needed rule to `_log/FORGE-PROPOSALS.md`. A human holds the pen.
